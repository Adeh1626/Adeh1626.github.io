What we do

Three connected services, one technical core.

Carbon accounting and decarbonization strategy form the analytical backbone; sustainability reporting turns that analysis into something a board, a lender or a customer can act on.

A

Carbon Accounting & Life Cycle Assessment

  • GHG inventories (Scope 1, 2 and 3) following the GHG Protocol, delivered as an interactive dashboard on request
  • Product and organisational Life Cycle Assessments (screening-level)
  • Materials flow analysis and circularity assessments
B

Decarbonization Strategy & Energy Transition

  • Net-zero roadmaps and Science Based Targets (SBTi) submission support
  • Reduction-initiative prioritisation and decarbonization ROI analysis
  • Feasibility studies for renewable energy transition projects
C

Sustainability & ESG Reporting

  • CSRD-aligned or voluntary simplified (VSME) disclosures for SMEs
  • GRI and TCFD-aligned reporting, ESG benchmarking and dashboards
  • Materiality assessments and screening-level climate risk review

How we deliver

Interactive by default, human where it counts.

Emissions dashboards and risk registers are built as shareable, filterable tools rather than static PDF decks — the same build-and-ship approach already proven through CircularBuild.

AI-assisted data cleaning and first-draft analysis speed up the groundwork, which frees time for the parts that need judgement: interpreting results, setting priorities, and defending numbers in front of a client or a board.

Target-setting, materiality calls, and what goes into a disclosure stay the consultant's responsibility — the tooling accelerates the work, it doesn't make the calls.
